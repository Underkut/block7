BLOCK7 Privacy Policy (English summary)

BLOCK7 stores the email you sign in with and the tasks, events, verses and settings you enter, only to show them to you and sync them across your own devices. If you connect Google Calendar, BLOCK7 reads your calendar list and the events of the calendars you choose, and writes only the events you create, edit or delete in BLOCK7. Data is never sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. BLOCK7's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Disconnecting in Settings › Account › Google Calendar revokes and deletes the stored Google token. Contact: in-app “send a note to the developer”.
